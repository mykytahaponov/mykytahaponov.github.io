Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

Hermes Personal Assistant is a privately operated, self-hosted assistant for the owner's personal and professional organization. It is not offered as a public application, and this website does not collect information through registration forms.

Google data accessed

With authorization by the Google account holder, Hermes may use read-only permissions to access Google Calendar events and calendar lists; Google Docs document content; Google Sheets spreadsheet content; and files available through Google Drive. Drive read-only permission may allow access to multiple files in the authorized account, not only one selected document.

Purpose and use

Data is used to show schedules, identify changes to events, extract relevant information from documents, prepare requested summaries, and generate reminders or task lists. Hermes does not request permission to edit or delete Google files and calendar events as part of these integrations.

Storage, processing, and disclosures

Credentials, task records, and information needed for scheduled operations may be stored on the owner's self-hosted server. Selected information may be sent to a configured AI model provider when the owner requests an AI-generated summary or analysis. Requested reminders and summaries may be delivered through Telegram. Hosting, model, and messaging providers may process data under their own terms. The assistant is not used to sell Google user data or serve targeted advertisements.

Retention and control

The owner controls access to the server and can stop integrations, revoke Google authorization in their Google Account, and delete locally stored credentials, records, and caches. Operational records may persist until removed by the owner. Revoking access prevents future access but does not automatically remove locally stored copies.

Security

Access tokens and credentials are not intentionally published on this website. This website contains no Google account data and no API keys. As with any self-hosted integration, the owner is responsible for keeping hosting access, software, and credentials secure.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The use and transfer of information received from Google APIs is intended to adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Contact

For questions about this privately operated project, contact its operator through the GitHub profile.
