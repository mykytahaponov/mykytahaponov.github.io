Terms of Use

Last updated: November 19, 2026

Hermes Personal Assistant is a personal, self-hosted tool maintained for private use. It is not a public subscription service and does not offer registration or access for external customers.

Functionality

The assistant may provide read-only access to authorized Google account information, generate personal reminders and summaries, and help organize tasks. Automated outputs can be incomplete or incorrect and should be checked before taking important actions.

Google authorization

The Google account owner grants and can revoke access through Google's authorization process. The scope of access is described in the Privacy Policy. This website does not collect Google passwords.

Availability

The service may be unavailable due to maintenance, hosting issues, API changes, or external service interruptions. No service-level guarantee is offered.
